Detailed advice

Understanding the work before you book

Before choosing to re-roof, it is worth comparing the cost and expected benefit with a targeted repair. Repeatedly broken or slipping coverings, defective battens or extensive underlying damage may justify wider replacement; a single damaged section may not.

A clear re-roofing quotation should describe the tiles or slates, breathable membrane or underlay, treated battens, ridge and verge systems, leadwork and any timber work included. It should also identify scaffolding, waste removal and protection of neighbouring property.

Building Regulations, ventilation and insulation requirements may apply depending on the work. The appropriate approach should be confirmed for the particular building before the project starts.

What to send when requesting an inspection

Send your East Sussex postcode, whether the issue is urgent, a brief description of the affected area and, if you have them, photos taken safely from the ground or indoors. Tell us about previous repairs or any existing workmanship guarantee. We will discuss next steps and whether an on-site visit is required.
