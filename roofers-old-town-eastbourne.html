Local roof advice

Practical roofing guidance for Old Town, Eastbourne

In Old Town, careful work around older chimney stacks, mortar joints and tile or slate details can be especially important. A small opening at a lead abutment may show as a sizeable damp patch indoors, so tracing the route of water is preferable to replacing roofing without evidence. Where older materials remain serviceable, matching and maintaining them may be a better approach than unnecessary renewal.

Before changing a visible roof covering on an older property, it is sensible to check whether any conservation, listed-building or other permissions are relevant. We can discuss the proposed materials and what would need checking; individual legal requirements depend on the address and proposed work.

If an extension has been added behind a traditional pitched roof, inspect the junction between the two roof sections as well as outlets and gutters. Leaks sometimes travel along timbers before appearing inside. Send pictures of both the internal stain and the outside roofline from a safe position if you have them.

Compare roofing options with a clear written quote

A written scope should say what is being repaired or replaced, what is excluded, how access is arranged and which materials are proposed.
