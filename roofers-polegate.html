Local roof advice

Practical roofing guidance for Polegate

In Polegate, the size and shape of the roof often determines whether a repair is straightforward or needs scaffolding and wider access. A slipped tile near a valley, a damaged ridge or loose lead at a chimney should each be assessed differently. We aim to explain which parts appear defective rather than treating every roof problem as a full replacement.

Strong wind and prolonged rain can reveal faults that were not noticeable in dry weather. After severe weather, watch for missing roof pieces from ground level, water spilling over gutters and new staining indoors. It is safer to photograph visible damage from the garden than climb up to investigate it yourself.

A written re-roofing quotation should distinguish the roof covering, weatherproof underlay, battens, verge and ridge details, and any necessary leadwork. A small maintenance quotation should likewise be clear about what is and is not included. Tell us whether your Polegate property is a house, bungalow, garage or extension so we can discuss the right next step.

Compare roofing options with a clear written quote

When asking for a quotation, explain the property type, the concern and whether it is urgent. A written scope should say what is being repaired or replaced, what is excluded, how access is arranged and which materials are proposed. We can discuss repair options before recommending larger work. A drone view is helpful only where safe, lawful and useful to the inspection.
