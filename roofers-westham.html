Local roof advice

Practical roofing guidance for Westham

Westham roofs vary by building age and construction, and a proper diagnosis depends on the individual address rather than a general claim about the village. A broken tile might be an isolated issue, while repeated movement across a slope may indicate deterioration of the fixings or supporting components. We can discuss a careful inspection of the covering and roof junctions.

Gutters and downpipes are worth including in the conversation when walls become damp after rain. A blocked outlet can send water across fascias or brickwork; a poorly fitted flashing may create a similar symptom higher up. Identifying the source helps avoid paying for unrelated work.

For a roof replacement or larger repair, request a breakdown of materials, safe access, disposal and finishing details. If you already have a guarantee on previous work, it may be worth contacting that contractor before commissioning someone else to disturb the roof.

Compare roofing options with a clear written quote

When asking for a quotation, explain the property type, the concern and whether it is urgent. A written scope should say what is being repaired or replaced, what is excluded, how access is arranged and which materials are proposed. We can discuss repair options before recommending larger work. A drone view is helpful only where safe, lawful and useful to the inspection.
