Willingdon properties sit across different elevations and roof designs, so exposure and access should be assessed on a property-by-property basis. A pitched roof and a flat-roofed extension often fail in different ways. Tile roofs can develop slipped pieces or defective flashings, whereas flat coverings may split around edges or outlets. We aim to review the reported symptoms before recommending a particular roofing system.
